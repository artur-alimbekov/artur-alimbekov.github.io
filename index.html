The process

How this actually works

Four steps. Zero jargon. You talk to me, not a ticket system.

  1. 1

    Send me your file

    Shoot over an STL or 3MF and tell me what you're hoping for — material, colour, deadline, vibes.

  2. →
  3. 2

    I send you a quote

    I'll look at your model, make sure it's printable, and reply with a price and how long it'll take.

  4. →
  5. 3

    I print it

    Once you're happy with the quote, I fire up my printer and babysit the layers so you don't have to.

  6. →
  7. 4

    Pickup or delivery

    Grab it at The Well downtown, or we can figure out delivery if that works better for you.

Skip the lab queue.

If you've ever waited two weeks for the campus print lab while your crit is tomorrow — same. University and college labs mean long wait times, limited material choices, and a queue you can't see the end of.

I'm the fast, flexible, one-person alternative: quick turnaround, seven materials to pick from, and you talk directly to the person running the printer. No forms. No mystery status page. Just me, replying to your email like a fellow student who happens to have a really nice setup.
